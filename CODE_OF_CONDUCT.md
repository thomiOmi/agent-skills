# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want this project to be a welcoming and constructive place for people with
different backgrounds and levels of experience. Participants are expected to
communicate respectfully, assume good faith, and focus criticism on ideas,
code, and behavior rather than people.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific in issues, reviews, and discussions.
- Welcome questions and explain decisions without gatekeeping.
- Acknowledge mistakes and help resolve disagreements constructively.
- Protect private information and do not publish secrets or personal data.
- Keep project discussions relevant to the repository and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Deliberate disruption, trolling, or bad-faith participation.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sharing credentials, exploit details, or other sensitive material publicly.
- Any conduct that would make a reasonable participant feel unsafe or unwelcome.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through GitHub.
Do not create a public issue for a sensitive conduct report. Reports will be
reviewed as confidentially as practical, and the maintainer may remove
content, restrict participation, or close contributions that violate this
standard.
