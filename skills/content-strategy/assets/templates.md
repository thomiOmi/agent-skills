# Content Strategy Templates

## Blog Post Template

```markdown
# [SEO Title with Primary Keyword]

**Meta description:** [Under 160 chars, keyword + CTA]

## The Problem
[Describe the reader's pain point]

## Why It Matters
[Context and statistics]

## The Solution
[Step-by-step or explanation]

## Key Takeaways
- [3-5 actionable points]

## Call to Action
[What to do next]
```

## Landing Page Template

```markdown
# [Headline: Value proposition in one line]

**Subheadline:** [Supporting detail or social proof]

**Hero CTA:** [Action button]

## What You Get
- Benefit 1
- Benefit 2
- Benefit 3

## How It Works
1. Step one
2. Step two
3. Step three

## Social Proof
[Testimonial or stat]

## Final CTA
[Repeat primary action]
```

## Email Template

```markdown
**Subject:** [Under 50 chars, value-driven]
**Preview:** [Under 100 chars, complements subject]

Hi [Name],

[Single message -  one idea per email]

[Supporting detail or story]

[CTA button or link]

Thanks,
[Brand name]
```
