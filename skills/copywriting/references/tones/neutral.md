# Neutral Tone

Voice characteristics: objective, balanced, unemotional, factual.

## Recommended Sentence Length

Medium. Balanced between short and long sentences. Focus on clarity over rhythm.

## Vocabulary to Use

- Neutral descriptors: indicates, shows, demonstrates, suggests
- Factual terms: data, evidence, results, findings
- Balanced qualifiers: somewhat, generally, typically, usually

## Vocabulary to Avoid

- Emotional intensifiers ("amazing", "terrible", "wonderful")
- Persuasive language ("you must", "you should", "guaranteed")
- Emotional adjectives ("outstanding", "disastrous")

## Do's

- Present facts without bias
- Use balanced language
- Present multiple perspectives when relevant
- Focus on what is, not what should be

## Don'ts

- Don't use emotional language
- Don't try to persuade or convince
- Don't use superlatives ("best", "worst", "most")
- Don't express personal opinions

## Before/After Examples

**Before (persuasive):**
This amazing product will completely transform your life!

**After (neutral):**
This product includes features that may improve workflow efficiency based on user testing data.
