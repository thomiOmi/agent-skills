# Casual Tone

Voice characteristics: friendly, relaxed, conversational, approachable.

## Recommended Sentence Length

Short to medium. Mix sentence lengths for natural rhythm. Avoid overly formal sentence structures.

## Vocabulary to Use

- Everyday language, common phrases, contractions ("don't", "can't", "we're")
- Friendly terms: hi, thanks, check out, explore, discover
- Relatable examples and metaphors

## Vocabulary to Avoid

- Overly formal terms ("pursuant to", "heretofore", "wherein")
- Stiff corporate speak ("synergy", "leverage", "paradigm shift")
- Complex academic language

## Do's

- Use contractions for natural flow
- Address the reader directly ("you")
- Keep it friendly and approachable
- Show personality

## Don'ts

- Don't be overly formal or stiff
- Don't use jargon without explanation
- Don't be too brief or curt
- Don't use passive voice unnecessarily

## Before/After Examples

**Before (formal):**
We are pleased to inform you that our product launch has been completed successfully.

**After (casual):**
Hey! Our new product is live. Check it out and let us know what you think!
