# Tone Adjustment

Overview of all writing tones for copywriting. Select the tone that best matches your audience and purpose.

| Tone | Best For | Characteristics |
|------|----------|-----------------|
| Formal | Business reports, official documents, corporate communications | Confident, concise, structured |
| Casual | Social media, blog posts, informal communications | Friendly, relaxed, conversational |
| Neutral | Technical documentation, user guides, objective reporting | Objective, balanced, unemotional |
| Informative | News, announcements, educational content | Fact-focused, clear, direct |

Load the specific tone file below for detailed rules per tone.

- [Formal](formal.md) - Confident and concise tone for business contexts
- [Casual](casual.md) - Relaxed and friendly tone for informal contexts
- [Neutral](neutral.md) - Objective and balanced tone for technical contexts
- [Informative](informative.md) - Fact-focused and direct tone for educational contexts
