# Informative Tone

Voice characteristics: fact-focused, clear, direct, educational.

## Recommended Sentence Length

Short to medium. Direct and to the point. Each sentence should convey one clear fact.

## Vocabulary to Use

- Factual terms: data, statistics, results, findings, evidence
- Clear connectors: therefore, because, since, however, moreover
- Informational markers: note, important, key point, summary

## Vocabulary to Avoid

- Flowery language, metaphors, analogies that obscure facts
- Emotional descriptors
- Ambiguous qualifiers ("somewhat", "possibly", "maybe")

## Do's

- Lead with the most important fact
- Use specific numbers and data
- Organize information logically
- Define technical terms when first used

## Don'ts

- Don't bury the lead
- Don't use metaphors that distract from facts
- Don't include irrelevant background information
- Don't express opinions as facts

## Before/After Examples

**Before (persuasive):**
You'll love how this new feature saves you hours of work every week!

**After (informative):**
This new feature reduces task completion time by approximately 4 hours per week based on user testing data.
