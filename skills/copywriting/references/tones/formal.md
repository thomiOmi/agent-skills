# Formal Tone

Voice characteristics: confident, concise, authoritative, professional.

## Recommended Sentence Length

Short to medium. Avoid overly long, winding sentences. Keep sentences focused on one idea.

## Vocabulary to Use

- Business outcomes, ROI, efficiency, results, deliverables, stakeholders
- Action verbs: deliver, achieve, execute, implement, optimize
- Professional terms: methodology, framework, governance, compliance

## Vocabulary to Avoid

- Slang, contractions where avoidable, emotional intensifiers ("amazing", "incredible")
- First-person pronouns where third-person is more appropriate
- Colloquialisms and idioms

## Do's

- Lead with the main point
- Use active voice
- Be specific with numbers and metrics
- Address the reader professionally

## Don'ts

- Don't use contractions ("don't" → "do not")
- Don't use exclamation marks
- Don't use emotional language ("I'm thrilled to announce")
- Don't bury the main point in long introductions

## Before/After Examples

**Before (casual):**
Hey guys, we're super excited to launch our new product! It's gonna be amazing!

**After (formal):**
We are pleased to announce the launch of our new product. The solution delivers improved efficiency and measurable business outcomes.
